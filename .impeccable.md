## Design Context

### Users

International brands evaluating or expanding their presence in Türkiye. They need a capable local partner for creator partnerships, social commerce, channel development, and day-to-day market execution.

### Brand Personality

High-end, professional, warm, and culturally aware. The site should make prospective partners feel confident in Asianode's local expertise while conveying the human, creative character of Istanbul.

### Aesthetic Direction

Warm editorial imagery anchored in Istanbul, with restrained, premium overlays and centered messaging. Preserve the existing light and dark themes; image-led sections must retain strong contrast and clear interaction states in both.

### Design Principles

1. Let local context and imagery establish credibility before decoration.
2. Keep message hierarchy direct: one clear proposition and two purposeful actions.
3. Balance polished professionalism with inviting warmth.
4. Maintain readable contrast over photography across screen sizes and themes.
5. Treat mobile as an intentional image composition, not a compressed desktop layout.
